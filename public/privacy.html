Privacy & data

What the beta saves

BRB Arcade saves your Twitch account ID, login, display name, game settings, active game answers, contribution scores, word bags, and the latest 200 Word Hunt round summaries. Twitch access and refresh tokens are encrypted on the server. Session cookies are used for sign-in; there are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Gameplay submissions are public in Twitch chat. Relevant game submissions are processed while your game is connected. General conversation is not saved as a chat log. The OBS source displays player names and game results. Keep its unique URL private.

AI judging

Word Hunt compares installed word vectors locally on the server. It sends no guesses to an AI provider. Krillion sends the round question and submitted answer text to OpenAI for judging, using temporary numeric answer IDs rather than Twitch usernames. Provider processing is subject to OpenAI’s API data policies.

Disconnect or delete

Disconnect on the dashboard removes saved Twitch credentials and browser sessions, stops chat access, and preserves your game records. Reconnecting restores those records. To remove your saved channel data, open “Manage saved channel data” on the dashboard and confirm your Twitch login. You can also revoke access in your Twitch account’s connections settings.

Encrypted daily backups are retained for up to seven days. Anonymous AI billing totals remain after deletion so the site’s spending limit cannot be reset. They contain no submitted answers or Twitch account identifiers. A recovery from an older backup requires the operator to reapply any deletions made afterward.

Beta operation

The service runs on Render, with private encrypted backups in Cloudflare R2. Public Twitch signups are enabled by default, with up to ten channels playing at once. The operator can switch the service to invitation-only access. For data questions or removal requests when you cannot sign in, contact the project operator. Viewers can ask the operator to remove a contribution profile using their Twitch login and the channel name.
