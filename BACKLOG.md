# Product backlog

## Krillion: shared questions and answers across communities

Status: consideration for a later phase.

Give different Twitch communities the same questions and build a shared answer
history. Use that history to reveal an answer's “depth” quickly, rather than
waiting for a new judging request to evaluate the entire round.

Potential directions:

- A rotating shared question bank or daily questions used across channels.
- Aggregate answers and semantic answer groups across participating communities.
- Immediate depth feedback based on prior responses, with results revealed at the
  appropriate point in the current round.
- Community comparisons or uncommon-answer bonuses after the round.

Open questions:

- What does depth measure: specificity, originality, rarity, or semantic distance?
- How does relevance remain a prerequisite for scoring well?
- How does scoring work for a question with little or no answer history?
- How do repeated answers, copying, and audience-size differences affect fairness?
- Should questions be synchronized across channels or simply drawn from the same bank?
- Which parts of the shared answer history are visible during a round?

This is a design exploration, not part of the current MVP implementation.
