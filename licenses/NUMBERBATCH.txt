BRB Arcade local semantic data

This data contains semantic vectors from ConceptNet Numberbatch 19.08,
by Luminoso Technologies, Inc. The bundled subset is a transformed version
(30,000 selected English terms, stored as 300-dimensional float32 vectors).
The transformed vector data is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
https://github.com/commonsense/conceptnet-numberbatch

Citation: Robyn Speer, Joshua Chin, and Catherine Havasi (2017).
ConceptNet 5.5: An Open Multilingual Graph of General Knowledge. AAAI 2017.

Frequency ordering uses the public-domain vocabulary of Stanford GloVe 2024,
exported by NeuML under the Open Data Commons PDDL. No GloVe vector values
are used for scoring in this version.
https://huggingface.co/NeuML/glove-2024-wikigiga-quantized
